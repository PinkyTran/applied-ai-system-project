# Flat-faced (brachycephalic) breeds

tags: brachycephalic, flat faced, pug, bulldog, french bulldog, boxer,
tags: shih tzu, persian, breathing, heat, exercise, dog, cat

Flat-faced breeds have shortened airways and cannot cool themselves efficiently
by panting. This changes their care routine in ways that override the normal
breed guidance.

Affected dog breeds: pug, french bulldog, english bulldog, boxer, shih tzu,
pekingese, boston terrier. Affected cat breeds: persian, himalayan, exotic
shorthair.

Walks must be capped at 15 to 20 minutes and split into shorter outings. Never
schedule a walk longer than 20 minutes for a flat-faced dog, even a young and
otherwise healthy one.

Walk in the early morning or after sunset. Exercising a flat-faced dog in heat
is a common cause of fatal heatstroke.

Facial skin folds trap moisture and infect easily. Wiping and drying the folds
is a 5 minute daily task and is medium priority, not optional grooming.

Flat-faced cats have blocked tear ducts, so daily 5 minute eye wiping prevents
the brown staining and skin irritation under the eyes.
