# Feeding schedule for cats

tags: cat, feeding, meals, food, kitten, wet food, dry food

Adult cats eat 2 measured meals per day, morning and evening. Each feeding
takes about 5 to 10 minutes including preparation and washing the bowl. Cats
are grazers by nature, so portion control matters more than meal timing.

Kittens under 6 months need 3 to 4 meals per day. Kittens 6 to 12 months move
to 3 meals per day.

Wet food must not be left out for more than 4 hours, so a wet-food meal needs
a follow-up 5 minute bowl pickup if the cat does not finish it.

Cats need a water source away from the food bowl. Refreshing water is a
2 minute task and can be folded into the morning feeding rather than
scheduled separately.
