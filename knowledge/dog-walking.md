# Daily walking for adult dogs

tags: dog, walk, exercise, adult, energy, breed

Most healthy adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of walking per day, usually split
across two outings so the dog is not holding its bladder for long stretches.

Guideline by energy level:

- Low-energy breeds (basset hound, bulldog, shih tzu): 2 walks of 15 minutes.
- Medium-energy breeds (labrador, beagle, corgi, most mixed breeds): 2 walks of
  20 to 30 minutes.
- High-energy breeds (border collie, husky, german shepherd, jack russell):
  2 walks of 30 to 45 minutes, plus off-leash running where possible.

A short 5 minute backyard or curbside potty break is not a walk and does not
count toward the exercise total, but most dogs need 3 to 5 of them per day.

Walks are high priority: a skipped walk causes indoor accidents and destructive
behaviour, so schedule at least one walk before any grooming or enrichment task.
