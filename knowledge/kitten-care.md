# Kitten care routines

tags: kitten, young, cat, baby, play, socialisation

Kittens under 12 months need short, frequent interaction rather than long
sessions.

Play is not optional for kittens: it is how they learn bite inhibition and burn
energy that otherwise turns into night-time zoomies and furniture damage.
Schedule 3 or 4 wand-toy sessions of 10 to 15 minutes spread through the day,
with the last one shortly before the owner's bedtime.

Kittens should be handled gently for a few minutes daily so that grooming, nail
trims and vet visits are not frightening later. A 5 minute handling session per
day is enough.

Litter training is usually self-taught, but the box must be shallow enough to
climb into and scooped twice daily because kittens use it more often than
adults.

Kittens should never be left with string, ribbon or wand toys unsupervised, so
play tasks always require the owner to be present for the full duration.
