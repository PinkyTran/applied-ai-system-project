# Medication routines

tags: medication, meds, pills, medicine, treatment, health, dog, cat

Medication is the highest priority care task. It is short, it is time-critical,
and a missed dose can undo weeks of treatment. Medication must always be
scheduled before walks, grooming and enrichment, no matter how tight the
owner's time budget is.

Giving one oral medication takes about 5 minutes, including the wait to confirm
the pet actually swallowed it rather than spitting it out later.

Most daily medications are given with or straight after food, so a medication
task should sit immediately after the corresponding meal in the schedule.

Twice-daily medication must be spaced about 12 hours apart. If the morning dose
is at 08:00 the evening dose belongs at 20:00, not at whatever time is
convenient.

Eye and ear drops take about 5 minutes and usually need a second person or a
towel wrap for cats.

Never schedule a medication task as low priority and never mark it as optional.
