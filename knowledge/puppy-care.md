# Puppy care routines

tags: puppy, young, dog, baby, training, housebreaking, socialisation

Puppies under 12 months need frequent short activities rather than long ones.

Exercise follows the five-minute rule: about 5 minutes of formal walking per
month of age, twice a day. A 4 month old puppy walks 20 minutes twice a day.
Over-exercising a puppy damages growing joints, so never schedule a walk longer
than the rule allows.

Potty breaks are needed roughly every 2 hours while awake, plus immediately
after every meal and nap. Each break takes about 5 minutes. This is the single
most time-consuming part of puppy care and must be high priority.

Training sessions should be 5 to 10 minutes and repeated 2 or 3 times per day.
Puppies cannot concentrate for longer than that.

Socialisation, meaning controlled exposure to new people, surfaces and sounds,
is most effective before 16 weeks. Budget 15 minutes per day for it.
