# Triaging care when time is short

tags: budget, time, busy, priority, triage, skip, minimum, short on time

When the owner's daily time budget cannot cover everything, cut tasks in this
order rather than shortening everything evenly.

Priority order, highest first:

1. Medication and any prescribed treatment.
2. Feeding and fresh water.
3. Toileting: walks for dogs, litter box scooping for cats.
4. Enrichment and play.
5. Grooming: brushing, nail trims, teeth, bathing.

The non-negotiable daily minimum for a dog is 2 meals, 1 walk of at least
15 minutes, and 3 potty breaks: roughly 45 minutes. For a cat it is 2 meals and
1 litter scoop: roughly 20 minutes. An owner who cannot meet the minimum needs
a dog walker or sitter, not a rearranged schedule.

Grooming tasks can be moved to a weekly rather than daily frequency without
harm for short-coated pets. Feeding, medication and litter care cannot.

Two short sessions usually beat one long one for both dogs and cats, so
splitting a task is a better response to a tight budget than dropping it.
