# Litter box care

tags: cat, litter, litter box, cleaning, hygiene, scooping

Litter boxes must be scooped at least once per day, and twice per day in a
multi-cat home. Scooping one box takes about 5 minutes.

The general rule is one box per cat plus one spare. A home with 2 cats should
have 3 boxes, which means about 15 minutes of daily scooping.

A full litter change (empty, wash, refill) is a weekly task taking about
15 minutes per box.

Litter care is high priority and should never be skipped. Cats that find a
dirty box will urinate elsewhere in the home, and a sudden change in litter
box habits is an early sign of urinary illness that needs a vet visit.
