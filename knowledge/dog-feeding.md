# Feeding schedule for dogs

tags: dog, feeding, meals, food, puppy, adult, senior

Adult dogs (1 year and older) eat 2 meals per day, typically morning and
evening, about 10 to 12 hours apart. Each feeding takes about 10 minutes
including preparation and washing the bowl.

Puppies under 6 months need 3 to 4 smaller meals per day because they cannot
store enough energy between meals. Puppies 6 to 12 months move to 3 meals.

Feeding is high priority and time-sensitive: dogs do best on a consistent
schedule, so a morning meal should be placed early in the day rather than
pushed back when the schedule is tight.

Fresh water should be checked and refilled at every meal. This adds no
meaningful time and does not need its own task.
