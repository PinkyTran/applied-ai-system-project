# Grooming dogs

tags: dog, grooming, brushing, bath, coat, nails, shedding

Brushing frequency depends on coat type:

- Short coats (beagle, boxer, dachshund): 10 minutes once a week.
- Medium and double coats (labrador, husky, german shepherd): 15 minutes
  2 or 3 times a week, and daily during seasonal shedding.
- Long or curly coats (poodle, shih tzu, collie, maltese): 15 to 20 minutes
  daily, otherwise the coat mats and requires shaving.

Bathing is needed roughly once every 4 to 8 weeks and takes 25 to 30 minutes
including drying. Bathing more often than every 4 weeks strips the natural
skin oils and causes itching.

Nail trims are needed every 3 to 4 weeks and take about 10 minutes.

Teeth should be brushed daily for 3 to 5 minutes; dental disease is the most
common health problem in adult dogs.

Grooming is lower priority than feeding, medication and walks. It is the
correct task to skip first when the owner's time budget is tight.
