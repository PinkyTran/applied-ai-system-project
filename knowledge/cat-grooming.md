# Grooming cats

tags: cat, grooming, brushing, coat, nails, hairball, shedding

Short-haired cats need brushing about twice a week for 5 to 10 minutes. This
mainly reduces hairballs rather than preventing mats.

Long-haired cats (persian, maine coon, ragdoll) need daily brushing of 10 to
15 minutes. Skipping days leads to mats behind the ears, under the legs and
along the belly, which must be cut out by a groomer or vet.

Nail trims are needed every 2 to 3 weeks and take about 5 to 10 minutes, often
split across several sittings because most cats will not tolerate all claws in
one session.

Healthy cats bathe themselves and do not need routine baths. Only bathe a cat
on veterinary advice or when something harmful is on the coat.

A cat that suddenly stops grooming itself, or over-grooms one patch until it is
bald, needs a vet appointment rather than more brushing.
